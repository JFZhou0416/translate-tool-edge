The Lighthouse at the End of the World

Every evening, the keeper climbed the spiral staircase with a lantern in his hand. The lighthouse had stood on that cliff for two hundred years, and nobody remembered who had built it.

The vault beneath the tower was cold and dry. Inside it, the keeper kept a single wooden box, and in that box there was a letter that he had never dared to read.

A Quiet Arrival

On the third night of October, a small boat appeared out of the fog. It carried a young woman who said nothing at all, and she walked up the path as though she had done it a thousand times before.

  • She asked for a cup of tea, and for a place to sleep until morning.
  • She asked whether the vault was still locked.
  • She asked the keeper his name, and he realised he had forgotten it.
“The sea remembers everything,” she said. “It is only people who forget.”
